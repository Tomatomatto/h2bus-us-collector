  • Routes & Schedules
      • Primary Routes – 15 Primary Routes and 2 Combined Routes scheduled to service Flint and the surrounding areas.
      • Regional Routes – Direct round-trip connection to places outside of Genesee County.
      • Peak Period Routes – Routes designed to run primarily during peak periods with selected stops.
      • Your Ride – Designed for use by persons who are unable to use fixed route bus services.
      • Senior Shopper – Easy ride and shop service at many senior housing locations.
      • Ride to Groceries – Transportation for individuals to groceries stores.
      • Rides to Wellness – Accessible transportation to medical facilities outside the normal fixed route bus service.
      • Specialized Services – Organizations providing additional transportation services for the elderly and persons with disabilities.
      • Davison Trolley Service – Trolley service throughout Davison
      • Genesee Connect – On-demand rides. Right when you need them.

MTA is seeking qualified suppliers for the following solicitations. Check the RFP or IFB that you are interested in, complete the contact form, and when you hit submit you will be taken to a page to download the necessary documents.

All submissions will be reviewed to evaluate compliance with bid or proposal documents. MTA reserves the right to waive any informality in the request for bids or proposals: To reject any or all bids or proposals and to make any award which it considers being in the best interest of the MTA.
